# Ripe for Punishment

You are immortal and stuck in the pillory while the villagers pelt you with tomatoes. Follow the mouse to dodge, catch the occasional tomato to eat it, and survive five days. Every hit covers the screen with more splats.

Made for [Mini Jame Gam #59](https://itch.io/jam/mini-jame-gam-59) (theme: *Immortal*, special object: *Tomato*). Placed #5 overall out of 107.

Play it on [itch.io](https://half-mana-games.itch.io/ripe-for-punishment).
